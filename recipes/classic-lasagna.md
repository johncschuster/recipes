# Classic Homemade Lasagna

Rich, savory homemade lasagna layered with Italian sausage, ground beef, rich marinara, ricotta, melted mozzarella, and fresh basil.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Italian Sausage
- [ ] 1 lb Lean Ground Beef
- [ ] 1 large Yellow Onion (chopped)
- [ ] 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
- [ ] 1 jar (28 oz) Marinara Sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
- [ ] 12 Lasagna Noodles (cooked al dente)
- [ ] 15 oz Whole Milk Ricotta Cheese
- [ ] 1 Egg (large)
- [ ] 1/2 cup Fresh Parmesan (grated)
- [ ] 4 cups Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
- [ ] 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley & Basil (chopped)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Meat Sauce**: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the Italian sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic until fully cooked. Drain excess fat. Stir in marinara sauce and tomato paste. Simmer for 15 minutes.
2. **Mix Ricotta Filling**: In a medium bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, grated Parmesan, chopped parsley, 1/2 tsp salt, and black pepper.
3. **Assemble Layers**: Spread 1 cup of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Lay 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce. Spread half of the ricotta mixture, followed by 1 cup of mozzarella cheese and 1.5 cups of meat sauce. Repeat layer. Top with final 4 noodles, remaining meat sauce, and remaining mozzarella.
4. **Bake**: Cover loosely with aluminum foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake an additional 20 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
5. **Rest & Serve**: Rest for 15 minutes before slicing and garnishing with fresh basil.
