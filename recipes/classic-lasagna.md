# Classic Homemade Lasagna
Rich, savory homemade lasagna layered with Italian sausage, ground beef, rich marinara, ricotta, melted mozzarella, and fresh basil.

## info
* Takes about 70 minutes total (25 mins prep, 45 mins cook)
* Serves 8 adults

## ingredients
* 1 lb Italian Sausage
* 1 lb Lean Ground Beef
* 1 large Yellow Onion (chopped)
* 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
* 1 jar (28 oz) Marinara Sauce
* 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
* 12 Lasagna Noodles (cooked al dente)
* 15 oz Whole Milk Ricotta Cheese
* 1 Egg (large)
* 1/2 cup Fresh Parmesan (grated)
* 4 cups Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
* 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley & Basil (chopped)

## steps
1. **Prepare Meat Sauce**: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the Italian sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic. Drain excess fat. Stir in marinara sauce and tomato paste. Simmer for 15 minutes.
2. **Mix Ricotta Filling**: In a medium bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, grated Parmesan, chopped parsley, 1/2 tsp salt, and black pepper.
3. **Assemble Layers**: Spread 1 cup of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Lay 4 lasagna noodles over sauce. Spread half of ricotta mixture, 1 cup mozzarella, and 1.5 cups meat sauce. Repeat layer. Top with final 4 noodles, remaining meat sauce, and remaining mozzarella.
4. **Bake**: Cover loosely with aluminum foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake an additional 20 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
5. **Rest & Serve**: Rest 15 minutes before slicing.

## notes
* Can be assembled up to 24 hours in advance and refrigerated before baking.
